# Beta Testing Guide — Distinct Routes

Thank you for testing **Distinct Routes**! Your feedback ensures that our safety routing engine performs reliably for cyclists and mobility device riders worldwide.

---

## 🔒 1. Scope Boundaries: Feature Set Is Frozen

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Feature Freeze Notice (v0.2.0):**  
> The feature set for the current release is **FROZEN**. We are exclusively testing for **bugs, crashes, routing defects, and unexpected behavior**.  
> New feature suggestions will be parked into our v0.3.0 backlog.

---

## 🎯 2. What We Need Testers to Verify

1. **Safety Route Adherence:**
   - Verify that routes in your city avoid high-speed multi-lane highways and use quiet residential backstreets, cycleways, and shared paths.
2. **Address Search & House Numbers:**
   - Verify that entering addresses or using Map Long-Press correctly sets Departure, Detour, and Destination points without losing house numbers.
3. **Map Canvas & Overlay Performance:**
   - Test single-finger map panning, pinch-to-zoom, top card collapse (`▲`), and clear all (`🗑`).
4. **Favourites & Ride Journal:**
   - Verify that saved favourites load properly from the Jetpack Room database and calculate route trails in 1 tap.

---

## 🐛 3. Bug Reporting Protocol

If you encounter a bug or crash, please submit a report with the following details:
- **Device Model & Android Version** (e.g., Pixel 8a, Android 15).
- **Exact Steps to Reproduce:** Start address, Destination address, vehicle profile, and safety toggles selected.
- **Expected vs Actual Behavior:** What happened vs what should have happened.
- **Screenshot / Logcat:** If available.

Submit bug reports to:  
📧 **Email:** `distinctroutes@pm.me`  
🐙 **GitHub Issues:** [https://github.com/SelectDistinctAU/Distinct_Routes/issues](https://github.com/SelectDistinctAU/Distinct_Routes/issues)

---

## 🌟 4. Join the Distinct Routes Steering Panel

Are you an active cyclist, cargo bike parent, wheelchair athlete, or urban planner? We are recruiting community leaders for the **Distinct Routes Steering Panel**!

**Steering Panel Members:**
- Get early access to v0.3.0 pre-release builds.
- Vote on feature roadmap priorities (e.g., Elevation Profile Charts, Weather Badges, Surface Warnings).
- Guide accessibility standards for wheelchair and handbike routing.

To apply for the Steering Panel, email **`distinctroutes@pm.me`** with your riding profile and location!
