# Quick Start Guide — Distinct Routes

Welcome to **Distinct Routes**! Distinct Routes is designed to keep cyclists, cargo bikes, wheelchairs, and mobility device operators away from dangerous multi-lane highways and onto quiet residential backstreets, cycleways, and flat paths.

---

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Choose Your Vehicle / Mobility Profile
Tap the **Selected Vehicle / Mobility Device** dropdown at the top of the screen:
- **Standard Bicycle / E-Bike / Pedelec:** Cruising speeds 15–20 km/h.
- **Cargo Bike / Dog Trailer Mode:** Climbing weight penalty applied to avoid steep inclines.
- **Wheelchair / Handbike / Mobility Scooter:** Automatic **Avoid Highways & Avoid Steep Hills** rules enforced.

### Step 2: Set Your Departure & Destination
You can plan your route in **two ways**:
1. **Visual Map Long-Press (Recommended):** Long-press anywhere on the full-screen map canvas to open the 1-tap menu:
   - `▶ Set Departure`
   - `● Add Detour`
   - `■ Set Destination`
2. **Text Search:** Type an address (e.g., *"Clinton Avenue, St James"*) and tap the **Search Button (`🔍`)**.

> [!TIP]
> **Address Entry Tip:** Entering a street name and suburb (e.g., *"Hill View Terrace, St James"*) provides the most accurate quiet backstreet routing.

### Step 3: Calculate & Ride
- Tap **Calculate Route Now ➔**.
- The app calculates a quiet, safety-weighted route powered by BBBike.org and renders the blue path on the map.
- Tap **Share GPX / Route** to export or hand off your route track!

---

## 📈 Learning Curve: How Far You Go Is Up To You

* **Level 1 (Basic):** Point-to-point routing with map long-press.
* **Level 2 (Intermediate):** Adding **Detour Stops** to build custom multi-stop trails and loops.
* **Level 3 (Advanced):** Saving routes to **Favourites**, reviewing **Ride Journal History**, and customising safety toggles in **Settings**.
